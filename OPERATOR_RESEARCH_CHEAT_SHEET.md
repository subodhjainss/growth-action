# Research cheat sheet — candidate checks, not rules

Companion to INTELLIGENCE_RESEARCH.md; all action parameters remain UNVALIDATED.

| Observation | Useful next check | Avoid concluding |
|---|---|---|
| CTR declines | Same-ad trend, delivery/mix changes, CPA and purchase evidence | Creative fatigue automatically |
| Frequency rises | Correct window-level exposure, audience context, same-ad response | A universal frequency ceiling |
| ROAS improves | CPA, order-value mix, daily concentration, attribution revisions | Another budget increase will preserve ROAS |
| CPA worsens | CPM, click response, conversion-side evidence, recent edits | Creative is necessarily the cause |
| Spend is below budget | Status, constraints, bidding and delivery evidence | Raising budget will unlock spend |
| Spend tracks budget | Economics and observed intervention history | More profitable demand must exist |
| New campaign has few purchases | Evidence volume, attribution maturity, business risk | A fixed age makes it ready to scale |
| One creative dominates spend | Its performance, alternative delivery and history | Dominance itself proves fatigue |
| Before/after an edit differs | Simultaneous changes and matched-period comparisons | The edit caused the change |

Use business economics to judge acceptability, account history to judge unusual movement, and multiple measurements to judge possible cause. Keep numeric facts, causal hypotheses, and action policy separate. Research sources and limitations are in the companion document. No universal percentage scaling rule adopted.
